SES 619 — Postural Control

COP Lab

Paste center-of-pressure data (time plus ML/X and AP/Y position), filter it, and see the X and Y stabilograms plus the COP path with Prieto et al.'s (1996) postural-steadiness measures — including the 95% confidence ellipse — calculated and drawn directly on the plots. Runs entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded anywhere.

1. Your data

If your export has several header rows (file path, channel name, units, axis), delete all but the one row with the column names you want to use, so the pasted header is a single row.

2. Columns & filter

Tell us what each pasted column actually is — nothing is assumed from column order or position.

Postural sway COP is typically low-pass filtered around 10–12.5 Hz to remove force-plate noise while keeping genuine sway content — see Filter Compare if you want to check that choice against your own data.

Prieto (1996) postural steadiness measures

ML = medio-lateral (X), AP = antero-posterior (Y), Resultant = combined 2-D measure.

Measures follow Prieto, T.E., Myklebust, J.B., Hoffmann, R.G., Lovett, E.G., & Myklebust, B.M. (1996), "Measures of postural steadiness: differences between healthy young and elderly adults," IEEE Transactions on Biomedical Engineering 43(9), 956–966, using the formula summary in Quijoux et al. (2021), "A review of center of pressure (COP) variables to quantify standing balance in elderly people: Algorithms and open-access code," Physiological Reports 9(22). MDIST/RDIST use the mean COP position as origin; TOTEX (path length) and mean velocity use the actual per-sample time differences, not an assumed constant rate. The 95% confidence ellipse area is 2π×[(N−1)/(N−2)]×F0.95,2,N−2×√(λ1λ2), where λ1,λ2 are the eigenvalues of the COP covariance matrix and F0.95,2,N−2 is the 95th-percentile F-statistic with 2 and N−2 degrees of freedom (computed here from its closed form for 2 numerator degrees of freedom, rather than a table). All measures are computed on the filtered data shown (or raw, if filtering is turned off).
