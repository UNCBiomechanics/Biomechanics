SES 619 — Segment Kinematics

Segment Animator

Paste marker coordinate data, define segments from marker pairs, and watch the reconstructed skeleton move through the trial — with each segment's length and angle updating live as you play. A rigid segment's length should stay essentially constant frame to frame; if it drifts, that's a marker-tracking problem, not a movement one. Runs entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Column naming rule: everything after the last underscore is the axis (X, Y, or Z); everything before it is the marker name. So RASIS_X and RASIS_Z are two axes of the same marker, RASIS.

Axes and Markers

Pick which two of your captured axes to view as a 2-D projection (the usual choice for sagittal-plane gait data is the direction-of-travel axis as horizontal and the vertical axis as vertical).

Segments

A segment is a rigid line between two markers. Common leg markers here auto-populate thigh/shank/foot if present — add, edit, or remove as needed.
