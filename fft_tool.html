FFT Quick-Look

Paste a column of signal values, or two columns of time and value, and see its frequency content immediately. Runs entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded anywhere.

One column = amplitude values only (set the sampling rate below). Two columns = time and value (sampling rate is worked out automatically from the time column).

Time domain

Frequency domain (amplitude spectrum)

Notes for interpreting the result: data is zero-padded up to the next power of two before transforming, which smooths the frequency axis but does not add information — amplitude is still scaled by your actual sample count. Unless a frequency happens to complete a whole number of cycles in your recording, its energy will spread across a couple of neighboring bins rather than landing in one exactly (spectral leakage) — that's expected, not an error, and it's the same behavior you'd see in MATLAB, Python, or any other FFT. If your two-column paste has uneven time spacing, the sampling rate is estimated from the median spacing; very uneven sampling will distort the result, since the FFT assumes even spacing.
